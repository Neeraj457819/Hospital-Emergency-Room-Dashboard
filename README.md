# Hospital-Emergency-Room-Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

The Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard is an interactive data analytics project designed to analyze hospital emergency room data and provide meaningful insights into patient visits, waiting times, admission status, patient satisfaction, and department referrals.

The dashboard helps understand hospital performance through interactive visualizations, KPIs, charts, and monthly/yearly filters.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze the total number of patients visiting the emergency room.

Monitor average patient waiting time.

Evaluate patient satisfaction scores.

Compare admitted and non-admitted patients.

Analyze patient age distribution.

Measure the percentage of patients attended within 30 minutes.

Understand patient distribution by gender.

Identify the most frequently referred departments.

📊 Key Dashboard Features

1. Number of Patients

Displays the total number of patients visiting the emergency room with daily trends.

2. Average Wait Time

Tracks the average time patients wait before receiving medical attention.

3. Patient Satisfaction Score

Analyzes patient satisfaction levels to understand healthcare service quality.

4. Patient Admission Status

Shows the number and percentage of admitted and non-admitted patients.

5. Patient Age Distribution

Categorizes patients into different age groups for demographic analysis.

6. Timeliness Analysis

Measures the percentage of patients receiving medical attention within 30 minutes.

7. Gender Analysis

Visualizes the distribution of patients by gender.

8. Department Referrals

Identifies which hospital departments receive the highest number of patient referrals.
